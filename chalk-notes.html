Chalk Notes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

Overview

Chalk Notes is a personal chalkboard doodle journal. You draw quick sketches and save them into boards you organize yourself — Family, Ideas, Just for me, or any board you create. Chalk Notes is local-only: it is not a messaging app, and doodles are never sent to another person or device.

Information We Collect

Chalk Notes does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, or location, and there is no account or sign-in. We collect a small amount of anonymous usage information to understand how the app is used and to fix bugs:

  • Anonymous usage events (e.g. which screen you opened, whether a doodle was saved) — no doodle content, board names, or personal identifiers are included in these events.
  • An anonymous, randomly generated device/subscription identifier (via RevenueCat) used only to manage optional subscriptions.
  • If you choose to submit a bug report from within the app, a screenshot and short description you provide are sent to our support inbox so we can fix the issue. This only happens when you trigger it yourself — it is never automatic.

None of this data is sold or used for advertising.

Local Storage

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Your chalk doodles and the boards you organize them into
  • The doodle shown in your Home Screen widget
  • App preferences

Your doodles never leave your device except when you explicitly attach one to a bug report you choose to send us.

Third-Party Services

Chalk Notes uses RevenueCat to manage optional subscriptions (Apple StoreKit handles all payment information; we never see your card details) and a lightweight analytics/bug-report service for the anonymous usage events and self-submitted bug reports described above.

Children

Chalk Notes is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any data from children beyond the anonymous usage events described above.

Contact

For privacy questions: open an issue at github.com/aniltaskiran/chalk-notes-support

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
