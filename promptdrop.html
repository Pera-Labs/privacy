PromptDrop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

Overview

PromptDrop is a weekly catalog of AI image-generation prompts. You browse prompts, copy them to your clipboard, and paste them into your favorite AI tool (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Sora, Gemini). The app needs none of your personal data to do this.

Information We Collect

PromptDrop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account is required to use the app.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or usage data.
  • We do not use third-party tracking SDKs.

Local Storage

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Favorited prompts
  • Copy count and daily streak
  • App preferences (dark mode, notifications)

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

PromptDrop does not call any third-party APIs from the app itself. The catalog of prompts is shipped inside the app bundle. When you copy a prompt and paste it into another AI app, that app's privacy policy applies to that interaction — not ours.

Children

PromptDrop is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions: open an issue at github.com/aniltaskiran/promptdrop-support

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
