Frekans — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-26 · Bundle: live.radio.stations

Summary

Frekans is a radio streaming app. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising trackers.

What the app does

  • Fetches a public directory of radio stations from radio-browser.info, an open community-maintained API.
  • Streams the audio of the station you choose, directly from that station's public stream URL.
  • Stores your favorite stations and recently played list locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Frekans does not:

  • Create or require an account.
  • Ask for your email, name, phone number, location, or any other personal identifier.
  • Track your listening history on any server.
  • Embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Use cookies or device identifiers for tracking.

Network requests

The app makes two kinds of network requests, both initiated by your taps:

  • To radio-browser.info (and its public mirrors) to list available stations and countries.
  • To the streaming URL of the station you start playing. The station operator may log standard server metadata (IP address, user-agent) per their own privacy policy — this is outside Frekans's control.

Children

Frekans does not knowingly target or collect data from children under 13.

Third parties

Frekans does not share data with third parties because Frekans does not collect data. The radio stations you stream are independent third parties — their privacy practices apply to your direct interaction with their stream servers.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective date.
