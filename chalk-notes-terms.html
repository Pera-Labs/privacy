What the App Does

Chalk Notes is a chalkboard doodle app. Doodles you draw are saved to boards on your own device and, optionally, shown on a Home Screen widget. You may also pair your device with one other person's device using a one-time invite code to share a single board — once paired, doodles you draw sync to their device and vice versa. Chalk Notes is not a general messaging or communication service: a shared board is limited to the two paired devices, and there is no way to add additional participants, browse other users, or message anyone outside a board you've paired.

Shared Boards

When you create a shared board, you generate a one-time invite code. That code may be used once by one other person to pair with your board; after it is used, it can't be reused by anyone else. You are responsible for who you share your invite code with. You may leave or delete a shared board at any time from within the app, which disconnects it and removes its doodles from our sync service. We may disable a shared board in response to a report of misuse.
